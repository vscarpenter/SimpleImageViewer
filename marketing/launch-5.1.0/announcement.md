## X: @StillViewApp, seven-post series

Day 0 publishes when 5.1 goes live. Days 1 to 6 follow daily at 9:00 AM Central.

### Day 0: launch

StillView 5.1 is on the Mac App Store. Turn on Include Subfolders to see every image in a folder tree at once, filter by folder, and search by name or path. It also reopens right where you left off.

https://apps.apple.com/us/app/stillview-image-viewer/id6749210445

### Day 1: subfolders

Photos rarely live in one flat folder. In StillView 5.1, View > Include Subfolders combines a folder and everything under it into one collection. The folder tree narrows it to a single branch without reopening anything.

### Day 2: text extraction

You don't need Apple Intelligence to copy text out of an image. StillView 5.1 runs Extract text with Vision on your Mac, even with AI Insights turned off. Receipts, signs, and screenshots come out ready to paste.

### Day 3: session restore

Quit StillView and come back tomorrow. It opens the same folder on the same image, with your sort order, subfolder setting, folder filter, and search intact.

### Day 4: AI Insights

AI Insights descriptions are shorter in StillView 5.1 and stick to what's visible in the photo. Tall documents and receipts get a separate, higher-resolution text pass. Apple Intelligence runs on your Mac, and nothing is uploaded.

### Day 5: keyboard

Grid view now moves by row. Up and Down jump a full row based on how many columns fit the window, an outline marks the selection, and Return opens the image. ⌘R refreshes the folder and keeps your place.

### Day 6: big folders

Open a large folder in StillView 5.1 and images start appearing while the scan is still running. Cancel keeps everything it already found. It still works straight from your folders, with no account and no tracking.

## LinkedIn: Vinny's launch post

One folder at a time was StillView's biggest limit.

Version 5.1 fixes that. Turn on Include Subfolders and every image in the folder tree shows up in one view. A sidebar narrows it to one branch, and search finds images by name or path. Text extraction now works even with AI Insights off, and the app reopens on the image you were looking at. The last review before submission also caught a sandbox bug: Move to Trash failed in folders StillView reopened after a relaunch. That one alone was worth the review.
